Advisory is useful when the next move matters but day-to-day work keeps competing for attention. The engagement creates space to define the decision, examine what is known and organise a workable path forward.

The process

From a business question to an actionable path.

The process remains practical and evidence-aware without forcing every situation into the same template.

  1. 01

    Define the decision

    Identify the question, desired outcome, people involved and current constraints.

  2. 02

    Examine what is known

    Review customer feedback, existing data, operating context and relevant assumptions.

  3. 03

    Develop practical options

    Compare possible approaches without assuming that technology is always the answer.

  4. 04

    Translate the decision into action

    Structure priorities, responsibilities, measures and immediate next steps.

  5. 05

    Review what was learned

    Assess early feedback and refine the direction where needed.

Common questions

Before starting an advisory engagement.

A call can help distinguish whether the need is advisory, a defined project or a combination of both.

Who is advisory best suited for?

It is designed for SME founders and small teams with a specific priority, decision or operating challenge that needs structured thinking and practical follow-through.

What types of advisory engagement are available?

An engagement may begin with a focused advisory session, a defined short-term advisory sprint, or ongoing fractional growth lead and execution support. The format is shaped around the decision and level of involvement required.

How long does an advisory engagement last?

The period depends on the question and scope. It may be a single session, a defined engagement over several weeks, or ongoing monthly support. The cadence and duration are agreed before work begins.

How is advisory different from a project?

Advisory centres on clarifying priorities and decisions. A project centres on building or delivering a defined system, workflow or analysis.

What should I prepare?

Bring the question, relevant context and any existing customer, sales or operating information. A polished brief is not required.

Can Alyst Studio support execution?

Yes, where the execution scope is agreed. This may be structured as defined project work or fractional growth lead and execution support.
