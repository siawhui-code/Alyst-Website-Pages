The process

From operational friction to a usable handover.

The project stays grounded in the real workflow and the people who will use the outcome.

  1. 01

    Discover the friction

    Understand the current task, users, tools, inputs and points of failure.

  2. 02

    Map the essential workflow

    Separate genuine requirements from inherited habits and unnecessary complexity.

  3. 03

    Build the smallest useful version

    Create a focused prototype or working system around the agreed outcome.

  4. 04

    Test with real scenarios

    Check the workflow, outputs and human review points in context.

  5. 05

    Document and hand over

    Provide the working outcome, clear documentation and sensible next steps.

Common questions

Before starting a project.

A short call can help determine whether the need is a project, advisory support or a smaller first step.

What kinds of work can be automated?

Tasks involving repeated information movement, checking, document preparation or defined handoffs may be suitable. The workflow is examined before recommending automation.

Does Alyst Studio create customised websites or applications?

Yes. Alyst Studio works with trusted full-stack developer partners. Together, we form the project team for website or application work that requires specific functionality and a customised build. The team and scope are confirmed after discovery.

Will the project work with our current tools?

Where practical, the solution is shaped around familiar tools and ways of working. Tool choices are made after understanding the requirements.

How are human review and control handled?

Review points, responsibilities and boundaries are defined as part of the workflow rather than added as an afterthought.

What happens after the build?

The engagement includes a practical handover and documentation appropriate to the agreed scope.
