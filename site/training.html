Applied AI training

Build practical AI confidence through guided practice.

Practice-led learning
Real task→Guided build→Repeatable method

Training shaped around the work participants need to do.

Sessions are suitable for non-technical teams beginning to explore practical AI use, as well as business functions such as operations, marketing, HR and administration.

Siaw Hui is an HRD Corp certified Train-the-Trainer and HRD Corp accredited trainer.

Who it is for

A practical starting point for teams.

The session format and exercises are adjusted to the participants, their roles and the outcome the organisation wants to support.

01

Teams beginning with AI

Build a grounded understanding of where AI is useful, where human judgement remains important and how to get started responsibly.

02

Business functions

Explore realistic applications for operations, marketing, HR, administration and other workplace contexts.

03

Guided application

Move beyond a tool demonstration by practising a method on relevant tasks and examples.

04

Repeatable learning

Leave with practical prompts, workflows or next steps that participants can continue using after the session.

The process

From learning need to guided practice.

Each engagement begins with the outcome rather than a fixed list of tools.

  1. 01

    Clarify the learning need

    Understand the participants, their roles and what they should be able to do after the session.

  2. 02

    Shape the practical exercises

    Select relevant workplace tasks and prepare guided activities around them.

  3. 03

    Deliver and practise

    Introduce the essential concepts, demonstrate the method and allow participants to apply it.

  4. 04

    Consolidate the learning

    Summarise the workflow, reusable prompts or practical next steps participants can continue using.

Common questions

Before planning a session.

If your context is different, a short call is the easiest way to decide what fits.

Do participants need prior AI experience?

No prior technical background is required for an introductory session. The content and pace are adjusted to the participants’ existing familiarity.

Can the workshop be customised?

Yes. The learning outcome, examples and practical exercises can be shaped around an agreed organisational context.

What formats are available?

Alyst Studio can discuss a four-hour introductory session, a one-day workshop, a two-day programme or a customised internal format.

Can the training be HRD Corp accredited or non-HRD Corp?

Yes. HRD Corp-accredited and non-HRD Corp training formats can be discussed, depending on the learning objectives, audience and administrative requirements.

What tools will participants use?

The tools depend on the learning outcome and the organisation’s context. They are confirmed during planning rather than assumed in advance.
