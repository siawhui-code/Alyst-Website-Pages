How Alyst works · First principles

Understand what is true before deciding what to build.

We break a problem down to its essential facts, question inherited assumptions and rebuild the answer around the people, constraints and outcome that matter.

  1. 01

    Understand the friction.

    Start with observable facts: where time is going, what keeps breaking, who is affected and what the business actually needs next.

    Outcome

    A shared view of the real problem.

  2. 02

    Question the assumptions.

    Separate real constraints from “the way it has always been done.” Technology is one option, not the answer to everything.

    Outcome

    A clearer set of choices.

  3. 03

    Build from the essentials.

    Create the smallest useful solution around the people who will use it, then test it in context and improve it through practical feedback.

    Outcome

    A right-sized intervention.

  4. 04

    Hand over and keep learning.

    Provide a usable outcome, clear documentation and a sensible next step so the work can be understood, owned and improved.

    Outcome

    A system the team can carry forward.
